Delete your account

14 November 2026

In the app: open Settings, scroll to the bottom and choose Delete account. Your account, profile, week plan, likes, shopping list, pantry and feedback are removed from our database straight away, and the copy on your phone is cleared.

Without the app: write to us from the email address of your account and ask for it to be deleted. We delete the same data within 30 days. Use the developer contact shown on fudapp's page in the app store you downloaded it from.

Crash reports hold no name, account or device identifier and cannot be linked to you; they are deleted once they are no longer needed to fix a fault. Backups made by our hosting provider are overwritten on their normal schedule.
