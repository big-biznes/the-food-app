Privacy Policy

14 November 2026

fudapp plans your meals for the week and builds your shopping list. This page says what the app stores about you, why, and how you remove it. fudapp is published by Gabriel Chitarliev, who is responsible for your data.

What we store

Your account: your email address and your name. If you sign up with a password, only a scrambled (hashed) form of it is kept. If you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address and Google account identifier from Google, and nothing else from your Google account.

Your profile: your goal (lose, maintain or gain weight), gender, age, height, weight, activity level, eating preferences and allergies, eating-out day, the country and shops you buy from, and your settings in the app.

What you make in the app: your week plan and the changes you make to it, the meals you liked or disliked, your shopping list and your pantry.

Feedback you send from Settings: your message, kept with your account so we can answer you, together with the app version, your phone's model and operating system version, and the app language.

A copy of your account data is kept on your phone so the app opens quickly and works without a connection. Shopping reminders are scheduled on your phone and are not sent through a server.

Crash reports

When the app runs into an error it cannot recover from, it sends us a report: the error message, the place in the app's code where it happened, the app version, and your phone's model and operating system version.

A crash report holds no name, email address, account or device identifier, and we cannot tell whose phone it came from. We use it only to find and fix the fault.

What we do not do

No advertising and no advertising identifiers. No analytics or tracking of how you use the app. We do not sell or rent your data, and we do not share it with anyone except the service providers named below.

The app does not read your location, contacts, photos, or anything else on your phone.

Why we store it

To run the app for you: to sign you in, size your portions, choose meals that fit your eating preferences, and keep your plan and lists the same on every device you use. The legal basis is the agreement between you and us to provide the app.

Your weight, height, goal, allergies and diet can say something about your health or beliefs. We use them only for the purposes above, on the basis of your consent, which you give by entering them. You can change them in Settings at any time, and withdraw your consent by deleting your account.

Crash reports and feedback are used to keep the app working and to improve it. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in an app that works.

Who handles your data for us

Supabase provides the sign-in service and the database where your data, your feedback and the crash reports are stored. Google provides Google sign-in, if you choose to use it. They act on our instructions and may not use your data for their own purposes. Their servers may be in a country other than yours.

How long we keep it

Until you delete your account. Settings → Delete account removes your account, profile, plan, likes, shopping list, pantry and feedback from our database straight away, and clears the copy on your phone. Backups made by our hosting provider are overwritten on their normal schedule.

Crash reports are not linked to you. They are deleted once they are no longer needed to fix a fault.

Your rights

You can see and correct your data in the app. You can ask us for a copy of your data, ask us to delete it, or object to how we use it. If you live in the European Union you can also complain to your data protection authority, for example the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens in the Netherlands or the Commission for Personal Data Protection in Bulgaria.

Children

fudapp is not meant for anyone under 16, and we do not knowingly keep data about them.

Changes and contact

When this policy changes, the new text is shown here with a new date. If a change affects how your health or diet data is used, we will ask for your consent again before it applies to you.

Questions or requests about your data: Write to gabriel.n.chitarliev@gmail.com.
